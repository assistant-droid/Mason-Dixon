Welcome to

Mason Dixon Jiu-Jitsu

Mason Dixon Jiu-Jitsu was founded with one simple idea: martial arts should be accessible to everyone, not just fighters or athletes. We wanted to create a place in Chambersburg where people of all backgrounds could come together, train in a structured environment, and grow stronger in body and mind. Our mission is to help ordinary people achieve extraordinary results through martial arts. Whether you are a parent looking for a safe, positive outlet for your child, an adult who wants to feel more confident, or someone looking for a new way to get in shape, we are here to meet you where you are.

The Benefits of Training with Us at Mason Dixon Jiu-Jitsu

Start Where You Are. Build What Matters.

Most people do not come in feeling ready. They feel unsure, maybe a little out of shape. Some have never done a combat sport in their lives. That is normal. What matters is showing up. At Mason Dixon Jiu-Jitsu, we teach No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu and Muay Thai in a way that is practical, respectful, and grounded in real progress. We do not do fluff. We do not expect you to be an athlete. We work with people who want to move better, feel more capable, and train in a space that values effort over image. Show up in gym clothes. Try a class. Decide from there.

This Is Not a Gym. This Is an Academy.

We do not teach workouts, we teach skills. No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu teaches control and calm — how to manage pressure, protect yourself on the ground, and use technique over strength. Muay Thai teaches balance, timing, and striking power without forcing it. The training is real, the people are welcoming, and the results show in how you carry yourself outside of class.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need experience to start training?

No. Most people who walk in do not feel ready, and that is normal. Classes are built for complete beginners as well as experienced grapplers and strikers.

What should I wear to my first class?

Comfortable gym clothes are fine for your first class. Our No-Gi Jiu-Jitsu program is trained without a traditional gi, so athletic wear works well.

What ages do you accept for kids classes?

Our Youth Jiu-Jitsu and Kids Striking programs are open to children ages 4 to 13.

Is training safe for beginners and kids?

Yes. Classes are structured and supervised, with an emphasis on control, technique, and respect for training partners. Kids classes are carefully supervised with no uncontrolled contact.
